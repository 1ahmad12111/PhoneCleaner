Privacy Policy

Cleaner Neat: Clean Up Storage · Last updated: October 13, 2026

Cleaner Neat helps you find and remove duplicate photos, similar shots, screenshots, large videos and duplicate contacts. We built it so that your personal content never leaves your device.

Data we do not collect

Cleaner Neat analyses your photo library and contacts entirely on your device. We do not upload, store, sell or share your photos, videos, contacts or any information derived from them. We have no servers that receive this data.

Permissions

  • Photos: used to find items you may want to delete. Deletion only happens when you confirm it, and iOS keeps deleted items in “Recently Deleted” for 30 days.
  • Contacts: used only when you open the Contacts tab, to find duplicates and incomplete entries. Merges and deletions only happen when you confirm them.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple. We use RevenueCat to validate purchases and unlock Pro features. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID and your purchase receipt; it does not receive your photos or contacts. See RevenueCat’s privacy policy.

Local storage

The app stores a few settings on your device (for example, whether you finished onboarding and how much space you have freed). This data never leaves your device and is removed when you delete the app.

Children

Cleaner Neat is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any personal information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update this page and the date above.
